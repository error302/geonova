## Post 2 — The whole workflow, guided start to finish

> METARDU doesn't stop at drawing the plan. It walks a survey from setup to
> submission across six steps:
>
> 1. Setup — project details, UTM zone, datum
> 2. Field Collection — traverse observations and beacon coordinates
> 3. Computation — traverse adjustment and area calculation
> 4. Quality Review — closure and tolerance gates
> 5. Map & Deed Plan — the plotter above
> 6. Submission — the assembled statutory package
>
> Each step unlocks only when the previous one is genuinely complete, and the
> engine cross-checks the numbers so errors don't propagate into the boundary.
>
